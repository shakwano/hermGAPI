Hermes Agent

A private, single-user automation assistant.

Hermes Agent is a personal-use automation tool running on a private home server. It is operated by one individual and is not distributed, published, or offered as a service to the public.

The application connects to the operator's own Google account to read calendar events, keep personal reminders, and send notifications to the operator. It does not access, process, or store data belonging to anyone else, and no data is shared with third parties.
